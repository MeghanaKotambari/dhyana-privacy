Part A: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Dhyana is developed by Meghana Kotambari and published on Google Play as BORN2CODE. This Privacy Policy explains how Dhyana handles user information. Because Dhyana is designed from the foundation up as a 100% offline, private sanctuary, we do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information whatsoever.

100% Offline Sanctuary Guarantee Dhyana does not communicate with external servers, databases, or cloud backends. All features—including soundscapes, timer countdowns, pranayama breathwork, streak calculations, and reminder chimes—execute entirely on your local device hardware.

1. Zero Data Collection & On-Device Storage

Dhyana does not require user accounts, email registration, phone numbers, or passwords. The application creates no user profiles and collects no personal data.

The following application state is retained strictly on your device using local Android storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Session Statistics: Total mindful minutes cultivated, consecutive daily streak count, and last active practice date (used to grow the illustrative on-device Bodhi Tree).
  • Notification Preferences: Daily reminder toggle (on/off), chosen reminder time (e.g., 07:30 AM), and selected chime sound (Temple Bell or Singing Bowl).
  • Sanctuary Settings: Screen wake lock preference during active meditation and first-launch onboarding completion flag.

None of this data ever leaves your device.

2. Android System Permissions & Declarations

Dhyana requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to provide relaxation timers and reminder chimes. We never request camera, microphone, location, contact, phone state, external storage, or exact-alarm permissions.

Permission API Level / Type Exact Purpose in Dhyana
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ (API 33+) Allows the system to display gentle daily mindfulness reminder chimes scheduled by you. Optional; the app remains 100% usable if denied.
android.permission.VIBRATE Standard Provides subtle haptic feedback during breathwork intervals (inhale, hold, exhale) and meditation bell strikes.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Standard Prevents screen timeout during active meditation countdown sessions when the user enables "Keep Screen Awake".

No Exact Alarm Permissions: Dhyana does not request or declare SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM or USE_EXACT_ALARM. All reminder notifications rely on standard, battery-friendly scheduled local notifications.

3. Zero Third-Party SDKs, Analytics, or Advertisements

  • No Advertising SDKs: Dhyana contains no ads, banners, interstitial promotions, or advertising tracking identifiers (no Google AdMob, Unity, etc.).
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not track events, screen views, sessions, or engagement (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • No Crash-Reporting Backends: Crashes are handled locally by an in-app error boundary with a safe sanctuary recovery screen. No crash logs or device metrics are transmitted over the internet.

4. How to Delete or Reset Your Data

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device hardware, you maintain absolute control over it at all times:

  • In-App Reset: Open Settings > Reset Practice History. This instantly resets your minutes, daily streaks, and Bodhi Tree growth to day one.
  • Android System Clear Data: Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Dhyana > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently deletes all local app preferences.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Dhyana automatically purges all locally stored files and preferences created by the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Dhyana does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for general audiences and contains no social sharing, internet communication, user-generated content, or targeted features.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected with a renewed "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Because Dhyana operates 100% offline and possesses no contact channels to users, we recommend reviewing this public URL periodically.
